# G.C. Smart landing page plan

## Goal
Create a polished, mobile-first storefront landing page that turns local visitors into WhatsApp enquiries, phone calls, direction requests, and shop visits—without inventing prices, discounts, reviews, or product claims.

## Visual direction
- Use the selected **festive editorial** style: deep berry, antique gold, warm ivory, and a restrained deep green accent.
- Pair Cormorant Garamond display typography with Karla for clear everyday reading.
- Build an editorial, magazine-like composition with refined Indian festive details, crisp borders, warm metallic accents, and modest motion.
- Generate a cohesive set of editorial jewelry images for the hero and collections. Treat them as illustrative styling imagery—not authentic store inventory—and keep the real-photo/new-arrivals area ready for future shop uploads.

## Page experience
1. **Header and mobile navigation**
   - G.C. Smart wordmark, collection/occasion/about/contact navigation, and prominent WhatsApp action.
   - Compact mobile menu plus a persistent mobile contact bar for WhatsApp and calling.

2. **Immersive opening section**
   - Lead with “G.C. Smart” and the promise “Jewellery, Beauty & Style — All In One Place!”
   - Use a full-width festive jewelry image with direct actions for collections, WhatsApp, and directions.
   - Keep Bansberia location visible immediately to establish local relevance.

3. **Collection discovery**
   - Five image-led collection entries: imitation, gold-plated, oxidized, Dokra, and cosmetics.
   - Each category opens a pre-filled WhatsApp enquiry focused on availability and pricing rather than pretending there is online inventory.

4. **Shop by occasion**
   - Curated pathways for everyday wear, Durga Puja and festive looks, weddings and family celebrations, parties and birthdays, and gifting.
   - Occasion actions pre-fill the visitor’s WhatsApp message with the relevant request.

5. **Proposed Complete-Look Collection**
   - Present the offer as a clearly labeled promotional concept awaiting shop confirmation.
   - Show coordinated jewelry-and-beauty inspiration, complimentary in-store styling suggestions as a proposed inclusion, and bundle savings only as conditional—not an active discount.
   - Do not display invented prices; invite customers to ask for current combinations and prices.

6. **Brand and customer benefits**
   - Concise local-store story using the supplied facts.
   - Emphasize variety, style choices, multiple budgets, convenient contact, and products for everyday and special occasions without unsupported superlatives.

7. **Visual arrivals/gallery area**
   - Use generated editorial inspiration with an explicit illustrative note.
   - Reserve the structure for authentic product and shop photography when supplied; no fabricated testimonials, ratings, or sales counters.

8. **FAQ, contact, and location**
   - Answer practical questions about product availability, pricing, WhatsApp enquiries, categories, and visiting the shop.
   - Provide both clickable phone numbers, WhatsApp links, the full Vivekananda Road address, and a Google Maps directions link built from the verified address query.
   - Finish with a strong contact band and clear business details.

## Search and quality
- Add unique page metadata for jewelry, accessories, Dokra, cosmetics, Bansberia, and Hooghly search intent, plus social sharing metadata that uses only an appropriate image the page actually displays.
- Add semantic local-business structured data using only the supplied name, address, phone numbers, and product categories.
- Use accessible landmarks, headings, alt text, focus states, reduced-motion support, and touch-friendly controls.
- Optimize generated imagery and use stable responsive image dimensions to keep the page fast and visually steady.
- Update the site icon treatment to match the new identity where practical.

## Technical notes
- Replace the current blank home screen at `/` with the complete one-page experience.
- Define all palette, typography, spacing, and surface roles through the shared design system; reuse the existing button and accordion controls.
- Keep this version frontend-only: calls, WhatsApp, navigation, and map directions use direct links and require no account, checkout, or stored customer data.
- Add a focused test for the exact contact links and conditional offer wording, then verify the page at desktop and mobile widths, along with the current preview diagnostics.
